Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

PlayBand is a football play designer made by Trevi Web Co. in Birmingham, Michigan. This page explains what we collect when you use PlayBand at sports.treviweb.com, why we collect it, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

  • If you use PlayBand without an account: your plays are stored only in your own browser. We do not receive them.
  • If you create an account: your email address, and a password that is stored in scrambled form that we cannot read. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture link, and we never see your Google password.
  • Your playbook: the plays, formations, playbook name, player names, notes and team logo you add are saved to your account so they follow you between devices.
  • Share links: if you create a share link, a view-only copy of your playbook is stored so that anyone who has the link can see it. You can turn the link off at any time under Account.
  • Feedback: the message you send, your email address if you give one, and basic details about your browser and screen size so we can reproduce problems.
  • Usage statistics: we use Google Analytics to count visits and actions such as creating a play or printing. It uses cookies and collects general device and location information. We do not send the contents of your plays or your share links to Google Analytics.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your playbook saved and in sync.
  • To send account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.
  • To understand which features are used and to fix problems.
  • To reply to feedback when you ask for a reply.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads in PlayBand.

Amazon affiliate links

Our Coach gear page links to products on Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, PlayBand earns from qualifying purchases. When you click one of those links you leave PlayBand, and Amazon may set cookies in your browser to know the visit came from us. Amazon's own privacy notice covers what happens on Amazon. We never see what you buy or anything about your Amazon account.

Companies that help us run PlayBand

  • Supabase stores accounts, playbooks, share links and feedback.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website.
  • Resend delivers account emails.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and the optional Google sign-in.

These companies process information for us so that PlayBand works. They may store it in the United States.

Player names and children

PlayBand is built for coaches and other adults. It is not meant for children under 13 to create accounts. If you add player names to your plays, we suggest first names or nicknames only, and remember that a share link shows those names to anyone who has the link if you choose to include them.

Your choices

  • You can use PlayBand without an account.
  • You can download a backup of your playbook at any time from Back up / Restore.
  • You can stop a share link at any time under Account.
  • To delete your account and everything saved with it, or to ask what we hold about you, email info@treviweb.com from the address on your account.

Changes

PlayBand is in beta and this policy may change as the product grows. We will update the date at the top when it does.
