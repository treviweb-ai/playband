    Welcome to PlayBand

    Draw football plays, then print wristbands and coach sheets. PlayBand is free while it is in beta. Here is how it works:

    1. Start a play. Tap + New play, pick your game type (5v5 flag, 7v7 or 11-man) and a starting formation.
    2. Line up. Drag players where you want them. Tap Save as formation above the field to reuse that lineup on other plays.
    3. Add routes. Tap a player and pick a ready-made route like Slant, Out or Post. Or tap Draw, tap a player, and tap points on the field to draw your own.
    4. Print. Print sheets gives you play sheets, wristbands and a coach call sheet with matching numbers.
    5. Save. Tap Sign in and create a free account. Your plays then save online and follow you to any phone or computer. Without an account they stay in this browser only.

    Your plays save automatically in this browser on this device. Undo and redo are in the toolbar (Ctrl/Cmd Z). This is a beta, so tell us what feels clumsy or missing: info@treviweb.com
