How it works

From idea to wristband in three steps

  1. Draw

    Pick a formation and drag players into place. Tap a player and choose a ready-made route like Slant, Out or Post, or draw your own.

  2. Print

    Choose the plays for this week. Print wristband inserts in your size, play sheets for practice, and a coach call sheet where every number matches.

  3. Share

    Send players and parents a view-only link. They can watch each play run on their phone before practice.

Wristbands

Inserts that fit the band you already own

Print on plain paper, cut on the dashed line and slide it in.

  • Youth 3 by 2 inch up to QB and coach 5 by 3 inch, or a custom size
  • Diagrams or a numbered play list, in the grid you pick
  • Leave plays off for a game and save that selection for next time
  • Coach call sheet with the same numbers the players see

On the sideline

Built for the phone in your pocket

Nothing to install. Open it in the browser and fix a play between drills.

  • Big touch targets for dragging players and picking routes
  • Press Run play to watch the routes develop, with pre-snap motion
  • A free account keeps your plays in sync across devices

What you get

Everything a volunteer coach needs

  • Starter plays

    19 proven plays for flag, 7v7 and tackle. Add one, then make it yours.

  • 12 ready-made routes

    Tap once to assign a route. Tap again to flip its direction.

  • Formations

    Standard sets for every game type, plus your own saved lineups.

  • Defenses

    Drop in a front or coverage to show players what they will see.

  • Play animation

    Run any play at half, normal or double speed.

  • Player names

    Put a name on each position so kids find their own job fast.

  • Team logo

    Add your logo to printed play sheets and the coach sheet.

  • Save as image

    Send a single play to the group chat as a picture.

Questions

Good to know

Is PlayBand free?

Yes. PlayBand is free while it is in beta. Every feature on this page is included.

Do I need an account?

No. You can draw and print without one, and your plays stay in your browser. A free account saves your plays online so they follow you to any phone or computer.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. PlayBand runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install.

What wristband sizes can I print?

Youth 3 by 2 inch up to QB and coach 5 by 3 inch, plus a custom size. You choose the grid, and print diagrams or a play list.

Which kinds of football does it cover?

5v5 flag, 7v7 and 11-man tackle, each with its own field, formations and starter plays.

Who makes it?
